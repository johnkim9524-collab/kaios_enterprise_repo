# Dual-Vertical Operating Charter

## Purpose

Establish the operating model for two autonomous intelligence verticals under Intelligence Holdings.

- Kidults — The Autonomous Collectibles Intelligence Platform
- Artfund — The Autonomous Art Investment Intelligence Platform
- KAIOS — The Autonomous Intelligence Operating System

## Strategic Principle

Autonomy is infrastructure, not differentiation. Each vertical must create authoritative, proprietary, institutional-grade intelligence products that are difficult to compare, substitute, or reproduce.

## Track A — Kidults Production Stability

Kidults enters an operate, stabilize, validate, and accumulate phase.

Primary priorities:

1. Production deployment
2. External smoke certification
3. Scheduler and collector stability
4. Kidult 100 Index data trust
5. Monthly Intelligence Report continuity
6. Historical archive accumulation
7. Backup and recovery rehearsal
8. Thirty-day operational baseline

## Track B — Artfund Autonomous Foundation

Artfund enters a define, model, validate, and build phase.

Primary priorities:

1. Product charter
2. Information architecture
3. Data Dictionary v1
4. Source Registry v1
5. Provenance and rights policy
6. Index Methodology v1
7. Report and archive framework
8. KAIOS Artfund domain-pack contract
9. Luxury and institutional product design system
10. Repository creation gate

## Architecture Boundary

Shared KAIOS capabilities:

- scheduling
- collection framework
- normalization framework
- authentication and RBAC
- observability
- backup and recovery
- release governance

Vertical-specific capabilities:

- source definitions
- entity models
- scoring methodology
- index methodology
- editorial interpretation
- reports
- visual identity
- commercial product structure

Kidults and Artfund must use separate databases, credentials, logs, schedules, backups, and release records.

## Product Quality Standard

Every release must be evaluated across:

1. Product Quality Score
2. Data Trust Score
3. Luxury Brand Fit
4. Institutional Readiness
5. Irreplaceability Impact

A feature must not be released unless it improves authority, customer value, and defensibility.

## Initial Allocation

Until Kidults completes its production stability baseline:

- Kidults: 60 percent
- Artfund: 40 percent

After Kidults demonstrates thirty days of stable autonomous operation:

- Kidults: 25 percent
- Artfund: 75 percent

## Current Execution

- Sprint 17A: Kidults Production Deployment and Stability Baseline
- Sprint 17B: Artfund Autonomous Foundation and Product Quality System

## Release Gate

The Artfund implementation repository is created only after Product Strategy, Information Architecture, Data Dictionary v1, Source Registry v1, Index Methodology v1, and the KAIOS domain-pack boundary are approved.
