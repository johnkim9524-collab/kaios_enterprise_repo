# Kidults A13 Zero-Base Global-Standard Holistic Review

Status: Decision document

Scope: Brand, product, information architecture, visual system, interaction model, conversion model, accessibility and technical architecture

## 1. Executive conclusion

The current Kidults portal should not be treated as a final global product design. It is a valuable staging prototype that proved the product story, data hooks, archive, methodology and status surfaces, but its visual and technical system has been shaped by successive tuning layers rather than by one globally coherent product architecture.

The correct next step is not another cosmetic pass. The correct next step is a controlled clean rebuild of the public portal while preserving the validated data and conversion functionality.

The recommended direction is **Global Intelligence House**: a hybrid of institutional market intelligence, premium editorial authority and category-specific cultural expertise. It should not resemble a playful collectibles site, a luxury brochure or a dense financial terminal. It should communicate within five seconds that Kidults is the trusted global intelligence layer for collectible markets.

Recommended product statement:

> Kidults turns fragmented collectible-market evidence into decision-ready global intelligence.

Recommended primary action:

> Explore Kidult 100

Recommended commercial action:

> Request Enterprise Access

## 2. Global-standard benchmark principles

The strongest global intelligence products consistently demonstrate five qualities.

1. **Product value before brand decoration**
   The user understands what data exists, what decisions it supports and why the source is credible before being asked to admire the visual identity.

2. **One clear operating proposition**
   Leading platforms present a dominant workflow rather than several equal marketing messages. PitchBook emphasizes comprehensive data, research and workflow tools. CB Insights frames the product as a single source of truth for technology-market decisions. Artnet combines a price database with reports and analytics to turn market evidence into usable context.

3. **Evidence and trust are visible**
   Methodology, coverage, update time, confidence and provenance are not hidden legal details. They are product features.

4. **Editorial authority supports data utility**
   High-quality intelligence products explain what the data means, but editorial expression never overwhelms the user’s ability to inspect the evidence.

5. **Technical credibility is part of the brand**
   Accessibility, stable navigation, responsive quality, performance and maintainable components influence perceived institutional quality.

## 3. Diagnosis of the current portal

### What is working

- The Kidults name is memorable and internationally usable.
- Kidult 100 is a strong proprietary-product concept.
- The combination of index, monthly intelligence, methodology and public status is strategically differentiated.
- The current portal has a recognizable visual identity rather than a generic SaaS appearance.
- Existing data hooks, archive behavior, quality status and conversion flows provide a functional foundation.
- The editorial idea that objects become culture before market consensus is valuable as a campaign line.

### What is not yet global standard

- The first screen prioritizes a dramatic visual slogan over immediate product comprehension.
- The dark green, ivory, gold and lime system has become visually fatigued through repeated overrides and inconsistent semantic use.
- Bodoni/Didot-style display typography creates character but is not sufficiently robust across operating systems, languages and smaller screens.
- Seven equal top-level navigation items overstate secondary content and weaken the primary customer journey.
- Methodology and Status are important trust surfaces but should not visually compete with core product exploration.
- Newsletter, waitlist and inquiry compete as equal commercial choices.
- The stylesheet architecture is accumulated rather than canonical.
- Mobile navigation is dense and visually uneven.
- Staging and governance language appears too frequently in the customer journey.

## 5. Three strategic design directions

### Direction A — Institutional Intelligence Platform

Character: precise, neutral, data-first, highly functional.

Visual references: financial research interfaces, private-market data platforms, institutional dashboards.

Strengths:
- Highest credibility with enterprise and institutional buyers
- Strongest product clarity
- Best accessibility and scalability
- Easy internationalization

Risks:
- Could become visually generic
- May underexpress the cultural and collectible character of the brand

Estimated global-standard potential: 8.8/10

### Direction B — Global Intelligence House — Recommended

Character: institutional intelligence with premium editorial authority.

Visual model:
- disciplined data grid
- high-contrast neutral palette
- restrained editorial typography
- selective cultural imagery or graphic evidence
- strong index and report hierarchy

Strengths:
- Balances trust, distinction and commercial clarity
- Fits Kidults as both a data platform and an intelligence publisher
- Supports index, reports, archive and enterprise access in one coherent system
- Can expand into additional categories without looking like a toy marketplace

Risks:
- Requires disciplined art direction
- Must avoid drifting back into decorative luxury styling

Estimated global-standard potential: 9.4/10

### Direction C — Cultural Market Publication

Character: visually expressive, magazine-led, narrative and trend-focused.

Strengths:
- Strong public brand recognition
- Excellent for reports, features and community growth
- Distinctive within the collectibles category

Risks:
- Weakest enterprise-product clarity
- Data may feel secondary
- Harder to justify premium institutional pricing

Estimated global-standard potential: 8.1/10

## 6. Recommended direction: Global Intelligence House

The portal should feel like a trusted international research institution that happens to understand collectible culture better than anyone else.

It should combine:

- the decision clarity of a market-intelligence platform
- the evidence transparency of a research product
- the cultural authority of a premium global publication
- the restraint of an institutional brand

It should not combine:

- luxury decoration without functional meaning
- collectible nostalgia
- marketplace visual conventions
- financial-terminal density on the public portal

## 7. Proposed color system

### Core palette

| Token | Proposed color | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | `#F4F2ED` | Warm neutral primary background |
| Paper | `#FCFBF8` | Cards, reports and content surfaces |
| Ink | `#101418` | Primary text and dark surfaces |
| Slate | `#38424B` | Secondary text |
| Rule | `#D8D5CE` | Borders and structural lines |
| Signal Blue | `#315EFB` | Primary interactive and data signal color |
| Deep Blue | `#183A72` | Institutional emphasis |
| Positive | `#157A55` | Positive state |
| Warning | `#A66316` | Monitoring and warning state |
| Critical | `#B13A3A` | Critical state |

### Rationale

- A warm neutral canvas retains cultural sophistication without decorative luxury cues.
- Signal blue communicates analytical clarity and international technology credibility.
- State colors become semantic rather than ornamental.
- Dark mode can exist later as a product or terminal mode, but the public portal should default to light for readability, accessibility and report continuity.

### Color rules

- No decorative accent should appear without semantic purpose.
- One primary action color only.
- Status must use text, icon and color together.
- Report covers and cultural content may use broader palettes inside controlled editorial modules.

## 8. Proposed typography system

### Recommended stack

Primary sans-serif:

`Inter, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`

Editorial serif:

`Source Serif 4, Georgia, serif`

Data and labels:

`IBM Plex Mono, "SFMono-Regular", Consolas, monospace`

### Use

- Sans-serif for navigation, UI, cards, metrics and body copy.
- Serif only for selected report titles, quotations and editorial features.
- Monospace only for scores, identifiers, timestamps and evidence metadata.

### Type scale

| Role | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hero | 64–80px | 42–52px |
| Page title | 48–60px | 36–44px |
| Section title | 32–42px | 28–34px |
| Card title | 20–24px | 18–22px |
| Body | 17–18px | 16–17px |
| Metadata | 12–13px | 12px |

### Typography principles

- No compressed line-height for functional information.
- Display typography must not obscure product meaning.
- Font choices must support Latin, Korean and future multilingual expansion.
- The CULTURE treatment may survive as a report campaign or brand film element, not as the default product hero.

## 11. Desktop interaction model

- Fixed but compact 72–80px header.
- Clear active navigation state using weight or subtle background, not layout movement.
- Home page uses a 12-column grid.
- Index and report modules prioritize scanability.
- Cards should represent actual product objects, not generic decorative containers.
- Hover states must communicate action, not merely visual animation.
- Methodology and status open inside the same design system but as trust-center pages.

## 12. Mobile interaction model

- Replace the two-column seven-item navigation grid.
- Use a compact header with menu button and full-screen or sheet navigation.
- Keep primary CTA visible without covering content.
- Convert large tables into ranked cards with expandable evidence details.
- Use sticky filter controls only where they materially improve the workflow.
- Preserve 44px minimum touch targets.
- Avoid oversized display typography that pushes product evidence below the fold.

## 13. Conversion architecture

### Primary conversion

### Secondary conversion

### Contextual conversion

Discuss a specific market decision

The homepage should not show three equal forms. It should show one primary enterprise CTA and one lower-friction newsletter option. Inquiry should appear contextually on Enterprise and market-detail pages.

## 14. Technical rebuild recommendation

Recommendation: **clean public-portal rebuild, not another override layer**.

### Preserve

- data JSON contracts
- Kidult 100 bindings
- archive data and search behavior
- quality status data hooks
- conversion API and validation behavior
- staging isolation

### Replace

- current public HTML composition
- accumulated stylesheet stack
- page-specific shell rules
- repeated component markup without canonical ownership

### Proposed architecture

- `tokens.css` — color, typography, spacing, radii, elevation
- `base.css` — reset, document, accessibility
- `components.css` — navigation, buttons, cards, forms, status, tables
- `layouts.css` — shell, grids, sections, responsive behavior
- page-specific styles only when necessary
- semantic component contracts documented and tested

### Non-negotiable quality gates

- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Lighthouse performance target of 90+ for public pages
- no horizontal overflow at 320px
- keyboard-complete navigation and forms
- visual regression captures at desktop, tablet, 390px, 360px and 320px
- stable rendering in current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox
- no new production authorization

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Loss of current brand distinctiveness | Medium | Retain restrained editorial moments and Kidults naming assets |
| Functional regression during rebuild | Medium | Contract tests for all existing hooks and APIs |
| Scope expansion | High | Approve direction and component inventory before implementation |
| Recreating override debt | High | One canonical architecture and code review gate |
| Over-institutional design | Medium | Use cultural editorial modules within the disciplined system |
| Delayed production | Low | Production is not currently authorized; quality takes precedence |

## 18. Final recommendation

Approve **Global Intelligence House** as the preferred A13 direction and proceed to a clean staging rebuild.

Do not continue styling the A12 portal as the final product. Preserve it as a functional baseline and reference environment.

The global-standard target is not a more luxurious version of A12. It is a clearer, lighter, more evidence-led and technically disciplined product that can credibly serve international institutional buyers while retaining Kidults’ cultural intelligence identity.
