\# KAIOS 2.0 System Boundaries



\## In Scope



KAIOS owns:



\- Source collection orchestration

\- Signal normalization

\- Intelligence scoring

\- Confidence calculation

\- Intelligence writing

\- Quality validation

\- Publishing

\- Runtime health reporting

\- Machine-readable intelligence artifacts



\## Out of Scope



KAIOS does not directly own:



\- Third-party source availability

\- External marketplace integrity

\- Human editorial approval systems

\- Cloudflare account administration

\- GitHub account administration

\- Consumer authentication

\- Payment processing

\- External legal or compliance decisions
