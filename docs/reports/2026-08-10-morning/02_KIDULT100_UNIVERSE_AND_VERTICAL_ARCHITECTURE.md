# Kidult 100 Universe and Vertical Intelligence Architecture

Status: CANONICAL
Date: 2026-08-10

## 1. Benchmark thesis

Kidult 100 is not the 100 most expensive collectibles. It is the global benchmark of objects shaping collectible culture today and tomorrow.

Published size: 100.
Candidate universe hard minimum: 300.
Preferred operating reserve: 400-500.
Longer-term research universe: 1,000+ objects.

The published 100 must be selected from a materially larger and continuously monitored universe.

## 2. Current Core Verticals

1. Toys & Models
2. Watches & Jewelry
3. Automobiles & Mobility
4. Fashion & Accessories
5. Design & Furniture
6. Technology & Cameras
7. Gaming / Music / Screen Culture
8. Cards / Comics / Memorabilia

These are Core Verticals, not a permanent closed taxonomy.

## 3. Category expansion contract

A future vertical may be admitted when it demonstrates sufficient evidence across:

- cultural significance
- actual collector behavior
- structural scarcity
- secondary-market existence
- data observability
- cross-generation persistence

Examples of future candidates may include sneakers, rare spirits, sports equipment, architectural objects, vintage audio, industrial design, digital-physical collectibles, robotics, space memorabilia, AI-era hardware and creator artifacts. Admission is evidence-driven, not trend-driven.

## 4. Common Core + Vertical Intelligence

The architecture uses a shared common contract plus category-specific intelligence.

### Universal Common Intelligence Core

Every index-eligible object should support:

- WHAT / identity
- WHY
- value state
- scarcity
- liquidity
- momentum
- canon
- risk
- WHAT NEXT

### Vertical Intelligence DNA

Each vertical defines what actually creates value in that market.

#### Toys & Models
Edition/run, sealed/completeness state, grading/population, franchise longevity, reissue risk, secondary-market depth.

#### Watches & Jewelry
Reference, variation, dial/movement/case, serial range, completeness, service/polish state, condition, provenance, auction/dealer comparables.

#### Automobiles & Mobility
Production, specification, originality, mileage, condition, provenance, auction history, concours/institutional relevance.

#### Fashion & Accessories
Designer/creative-director era, season/runway significance, production, rarity, condition, cultural adoption, archive/institution presence.

#### Design & Furniture
Designer, manufacturer, edition, production period, provenance, condition, exhibition and institutional collection history.

#### Technology & Cameras
Generation, production/serial, technical significance, originality, completeness, working condition, design canon.

#### Gaming / Music / Screen Culture
Edition/release state, creator/franchise canon, format/region/variant, condition/grading, cultural persistence and historical significance.

#### Cards / Comics / Memorabilia
Issue/set/print run, variant, grade population, player/character/event canon, provenance, transaction depth.

## 5. Scoring architecture

Do not force identical raw factor weights on every category.

Stage 1: Vertical Score
- category-specific factor model
- category-specific WHY causal model
- category-specific comparables and condition logic

Stage 2: Cross-Category Normalization
- converts vertical scores into a comparable Global Kidult Score
- preserves category specificity while enabling one benchmark

Kidult 100 should use minimum diversification constraints, not rigid equal quotas. The benchmark must remain merit-based while avoiding domination by one category.

## 7. Required index anatomy

Each published constituent should eventually expose:

- rank
- movement
- Global Kidult Score
- vertical score
- confidence
- WHY
- supporting and counter evidence
- value state
- scarcity state
- liquidity state when available
- canon state
- risk/invalidation
- WHAT NEXT
- provenance/freshness

## 8. Adaptive benchmark rule

The benchmark taxonomy may evolve, but historical methodology, constituent history and category changes must remain auditable. New categories extend the system through registered Vertical DNA + Source Graph + WHY Model + normalization rules rather than ad hoc code branches.
