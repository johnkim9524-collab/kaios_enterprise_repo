# KIDULTS Future Portal Product Blueprint

Status: CANONICAL PRODUCT REPORT
Date: 2026-08-10

## 1. Product thesis

The portal is a collector and institution playground for discovery, research, conviction and decision-making. It should not change its information architecture whenever a new source, field or signal is added.

The shell is stable. Intelligence fills governed modules through stable data contracts.

## 4. Stable intelligence-card grammar

Cards may render different fields, but important cards should follow the same mental model:

- identity
- current state
- WHAT changed
- WHY
- confidence
- supporting evidence
- counter evidence
- SO WHAT
- WHAT NEXT
- risk/invalidation
- timestamp/freshness

## 5. Collector Mode

Collector-facing capabilities should include:

- discovery feed
- watchlists
- Kidult 100
- emerging icons
- object intelligence
- within-category and cross-category comparison
- valuation context
- scarcity/liquidity/risk context
- Collection Lab
- missing icons
- canon coverage
- acquisition/watch workflows
- governed alerts

## 6. Institution Mode

Institution-facing capabilities should include:

- acquisition relevance
- institutional/cultural significance
- provenance depth
- evidence export
- collection gap analysis
- preservation priority
- benchmark views
- custom research workspaces
- methodology and confidence traceability

## 8. UI change rule

New raw field -> no UI change by default.
New source -> evidence enrichment, no navigation change.
New signal -> enrich an existing module where possible.
New recurring customer decision -> product architecture review may add a module/workspace.

## 10. Design behavior

The portal should feel like a premium editorial intelligence terminal, not a marketplace catalog.

- stable deep-forest / rough-luxury identity
- authoritative typography
- high information density without noise
- WHY and evidence close to every material score
- confidence and freshness visible
- desktop/mobile parity
- no horizontal overflow from 320px upward
- touch targets and card stacking treated as first-class product requirements

## 11. Prototype strategy

The future prototype should exist before all products are live. Mock/data-contract content occupies permanent slots; real intelligence progressively replaces mock content without structural redesign.

Prototype changes require a deliberate product-level decision, not the arrival of a new dataset.
