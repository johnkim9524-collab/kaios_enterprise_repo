# Kidult 100 Universe and Methodology Outline v0.9

## Purpose

Kidult 100 is the reference market index for globally significant collectible products and editions.

## Index Objective

Measure the risk-adjusted market performance, liquidity, collector demand, scarcity, and cultural strength of a diversified global collectibles universe.

## Eligible Universe

A candidate must:

- map to a canonical Kidults Product and Edition
- have verified source evidence
- have observable secondary-market activity
- have sufficient price and liquidity history
- pass rights and commercial-use eligibility
- meet category and concentration rules
- have a reproducible confidence grade

## Exclusions

- purely digital assets unless separately approved
- unidentified or disputed products
- counterfeit-dominated records
- isolated asking prices without transaction evidence
- products with insufficient rights status
- assets subject to unresolved manipulation concerns

## Selection Factors

1. Market capitalization proxy
2. Transaction liquidity
3. Price reliability
4. Collector demand
5. Scarcity credibility
6. Brand and canon strength
7. Geographic breadth
8. Historical continuity
9. Source coverage
10. Manipulation resistance

## Weighting Outline

The index uses capped composite weighting.

Proposed factor weights:

- market value proxy: 25%
- liquidity: 20%
- price reliability: 15%
- collector demand: 15%
- scarcity: 10%
- canon strength: 10%
- geographic breadth: 5%

No constituent may exceed 5% at rebalance. Category and brand concentration limits apply.

## Price Input Hierarchy

1. verified completed transactions
2. verified auction results
3. trusted marketplace completed sales
4. approved dealer transactions
5. asking-price information only as a bounded secondary indicator

## Normalization

- convert to the index base currency
- separate buyer premium and tax where evidence permits
- normalize condition and certification
- identify bundled transactions
- exclude or winsorize anomalies according to methodology
- preserve original values and transformation evidence

## Liquidity Gate

Each candidate must satisfy minimum thresholds for:

- transaction count
- active observation months
- distinct trading venues
- median time between sales
- source diversity

Thresholds will be calibrated during backtesting and versioned.

## Rebalance

- quarterly scheduled rebalance
- monthly eligibility surveillance
- emergency removal for fraud, identity dispute, rights prohibition, or severe data failure

## Corporate and Product Events

Forks, reissues, restocks, edition discoveries, certification changes, and identity corrections are handled through explicit event rules and restatement logs.

## Index Calculation

- base value: 1,000
- base date: assigned after backtest approval
- daily calculation target
- total-return and price-return variants may be maintained
- deterministic calculation from version-pinned inputs

## Confidence and Publication

Every index point must expose:

- methodology version
- calculation timestamp
- constituent coverage
- price coverage
- confidence grade
- provisional or final status
