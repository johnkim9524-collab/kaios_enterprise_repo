# Kidults Portal Information Architecture v0.9

## Objective

Define a premium, evidence-led portal architecture for public authority, enterprise workflows, collector intelligence, and internal autonomous operations.

## Portal System

### 1. Public Intelligence

Primary purpose: establish market authority and external citation.

Required sections:

- Global Market Overview
- Kidult 100
- Category Indices
- Rankings
- Brand Intelligence Preview
- Monthly Reports
- Archive Preview
- Methodology and Standards

### 2. Enterprise Portal

Primary users:

- brands
- IP owners
- manufacturers
- marketplaces
- auction houses
- investors
- research and consulting teams

Required sections:

- Executive Overview
- Market Intelligence
- Brand Intelligence
- Category Analytics
- Competitive Intelligence
- Product Launch Intelligence
- Regional Intelligence
- Kidult 100 and Sub-Indices
- Report Library
- Saved Views and Watchlists
- CSV and PDF Export
- API Access
- Evidence and Methodology

Decision questions:

- where is demand moving?
- which brands and categories are gaining strength?
- how successful was a launch?
- where are liquidity and pricing risks increasing?
- what should an enterprise launch, license, acquire, or avoid?

### 3. Collector Portal

Primary purpose: improve acquisition, holding, valuation, and sale decisions.

Required sections:

- My Collection
- Collection Import
- Watchlist
- Price Intelligence
- Fair Value Range
- Liquidity Grade
- Scarcity and Canon Strength
- Alerts
- Auction and Release Calendar
- Personal Monthly Review
- Evidence and Confidence

### 4. KAIOS Operations Portal

Internal only.

Required sections:

- System Health
- Scheduler and Runtime
- Source Registry
- Rights Status
- Source Quality
- Collection Runs
- Entity Resolution Queue
- Data Quality and Anomalies
- Index Runs
- Report Runs
- Publication Status
- Incidents and Rollback
- Backup and Restore
- Stability Reports

## Shared Page Contract

Every intelligence page must support:

- updated_at
- source_coverage
- confidence_grade
- methodology_version
- evidence_link
- rights_eligibility
- empty state
- partial-failure state
- stale-data state
- desktop and mobile responsive rendering

## Luxury Portal Principles

- executive insight before operational detail
- editorial whitespace and disciplined hierarchy
- no generic admin-dashboard appearance for customer portals
- restrained motion and premium loading states
- charts optimized for reading, not decoration
- evidence available within one interaction
- mobile layouts designed independently, not merely compressed

## Navigation Boundaries

Public, Enterprise, Collector, and Operations navigation must remain distinct. Operational controls may never appear in customer-facing portals.

## Release Gates

- Luxury Brand Fit >= 95
- Product Quality Score >= 90
- Data Trust Score >= 90
- mobile and desktop pass
- no unexplained score
- no commercially prohibited source data
- no screen without real or explicitly labeled preview data
