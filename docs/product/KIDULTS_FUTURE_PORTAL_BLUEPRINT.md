# KIDULTS Future Portal Blueprint

Status: CANONICAL PRODUCT BLUEPRINT
Date: 2026-08-10

## Product thesis

The KIDULTS portal is a collector and institution playground for discovery, conviction, research and decision-making. It is not a page that changes structure whenever a new dataset arrives.

The UI is stable. Intelligence fills stable modules through data contracts.

Core promise:

KIDULTS identifies what matters, explains why it matters, and anticipates what may matter next.

## Homepage permanent modules

### 1. Global Radar
Cross-category movement and attention map. Answers: what changed today/this week?

### 2. Kidult 100
The flagship benchmark. Rank + movement + confidence + WHY + evidence.

### 3. WHY Now
The most important causal narratives behind material movements.

### 4. Emerging Icons
Objects not yet consensus but strengthening across scarcity, demand, canon or institutional signals.

### 5. Market Pulse
Vertical-level market state: momentum, breadth, liquidity, supply, confidence.

### 6. Canon Watch
Objects entering or strengthening long-term cultural/institutional relevance.

### 7. Object Intelligence
Deep object pages: identity, provenance, market state, scarcity, comparables, WHY graph, risk, outlook.

### 8. Compare
Cross-object and within-vertical comparison surface.

### 9. Collection Lab
Collector workspace: watchlist, collection quality, category concentration, liquidity risk, canon coverage, missing icons.

### 10. Institution Desk
Acquisition, collection gap, cultural preservation, benchmark and evidence tools for museums, archives, insurers, wealth managers and brands.

### 11. Research Library
Monthly intelligence, category deep dives, methodology notes, market events and thematic research.

### 12. Trust Layer
Provenance, rights, source class, methodology, confidence, freshness and data status visible throughout.

## Stable card anatomy

Every intelligence card may render some or all of:

- object/category identity
- current state
- change
- WHY
- confidence
- evidence count/source classes
- SO WHAT
- WHAT NEXT
- risk/invalidation
- timestamp/freshness

Adding a new raw field must not create a new card by default.

## User modes

### Collector Mode
Discovery, watchlists, valuation context, comparisons, collection lab, alerts and emerging icons.

### Institution Mode
Acquisition relevance, provenance depth, canon/institutional signals, collection gaps, evidence export and research workflows.

The same intelligence graph serves both modes; presentation and decision context differ.

## 8 Core + Dynamic Expansion

Current Core Verticals:

- Automobiles & Mobility
- Fashion & Accessories

They are not a permanent closed taxonomy. New verticals can be admitted when they pass cultural significance, collector behavior, scarcity structure, secondary-market existence, data observability and cross-generation persistence gates.

## Information architecture principle

Source changes should alter evidence, not navigation.
New signals should enrich modules, not fragment the UI.
New products should enter as governed modules or workspaces with explicit product rationale.

## Future commercial surfaces

- KIDULTS Public: discovery, selected Kidult 100, WHY, public research
- KIDULTS Pro: full benchmark, alerts, object intelligence, compare, advanced research
- KIDULTS Collector: Collection Lab, portfolio intelligence, acquisition/watch workflows
- KIDULTS Institutional: Institution Desk, evidence exports, collection-gap analysis, custom benchmarks
- KIDULTS Data/API: governed derived intelligence and benchmark feeds

## Design behavior

The portal should feel like an editorial luxury intelligence terminal rather than a marketplace.

- stable visual grammar
- high information density without visual noise
- rough-luxury / deep-forest identity retained
- typography hierarchy carries authority
- evidence and WHY remain close to every score
- desktop and mobile are equal product surfaces
- no horizontal overflow from 320px upward
- touch targets and card stacking are first-class requirements

## Prototype rule

The future prototype is a fixed shell backed by mock/data-contract objects. As real intelligence arrives, content populates the same named slots. Prototype changes require a deliberate product architecture decision, not a new dataset.
