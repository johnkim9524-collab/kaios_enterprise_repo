# Kidults Enterprise Portal Beta Contract v0.1

## Purpose
Deliver a luxury, evidence-linked enterprise intelligence surface for brands, IP owners, marketplaces, investors, and research teams.

## Required modules
- Executive market overview
- Kidult 100 index
- Brand Momentum
- Canon Strength
- Liquidity Grade
- Category Intelligence
- Reports and export
- Trust Surface

## Required Trust Surface
Every material intelligence component exposes confidence, source coverage, evidence count, methodology ID and version, rights status, freshness, and updated timestamp.

## Customer visibility gates
- approved rights required
- methodology status active or approved
- confidence score at least 70
- quality decision accept or review
- unresolved critical anomaly blocks display

## UX requirements
- premium editorial hierarchy
- no generic SaaS styling
- desktop and mobile delivery together
- no horizontal overflow at 320px
- explicit loading, empty, partial, degraded, unauthorized, restricted, and error states

## Release status
Staging beta only. Illustrative values must be labelled. Production promotion requires a separate certification gate.
