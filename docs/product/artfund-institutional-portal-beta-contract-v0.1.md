# Artfund Institutional Portal Beta Contract v0.1

## Objective

Define the first institutional-grade Artfund portal product surface using the certified Week 3 intelligence-engine foundations.

## Positioning

Artfund — The Global Standard for Art Investment Intelligence.

The portal is an institutional decision environment for family offices, asset managers, auction houses, galleries, insurers, appraisers, and research teams.

## Core Product Areas

1. Global Art Market Overview
2. Global Art Market Index
3. Artist Intelligence
4. Auction Intelligence
5. Provenance Intelligence
6. Liquidity and Risk
7. Evidence and Methodology
8. Report and Export Readiness

## Required Metrics

- Global Art Market Index
- Artist Momentum
- Auction Liquidity
- Provenance Strength
- Market Breadth
- Segment Rotation
- Confidence and Coverage

## Trust Surface

Every material metric must expose:

- confidence grade and score
- source coverage
- evidence count
- methodology ID and version
- rights status
- freshness status
- provenance completeness
- provenance dispute status
- updated timestamp

## Customer Visibility Gates

Customer display is allowed only when:

- rights status is approved
- methodology status is approved or active
- confidence score is at least 70
- evidence count is greater than zero
- provenance is not disputed for provenance-sensitive surfaces

## Portal States

- loading
- ready
- empty
- partial
- degraded
- unauthorized
- rights_restricted
- provenance_disputed
- error

## Product Boundaries

- No write API is authorized.
- Illustrative staging values must be explicitly labelled.
- Artfund Production readiness is not claimed.
- Public release requires a separate gate.
- Desktop and mobile are delivered together.
