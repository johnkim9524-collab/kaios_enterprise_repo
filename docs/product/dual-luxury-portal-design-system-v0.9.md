# Dual Luxury Portal Design System v0.9

## Objective

Define the shared premium experience system for Kidults and Artfund while preserving distinct market identities.

## Non-Negotiable Position

- Kidults — The Global Standard for Collectibles Intelligence
- Artfund — The Global Standard for Art Investment Intelligence
- KAIOS Operations remains an internal control plane and never adopts customer-facing luxury decoration.

## Shared Principles

1. Authority before decoration.
2. Editorial hierarchy before dashboard density.
3. Evidence is part of the visual product, not a legal footer.
4. Motion must communicate state or hierarchy and never exist only for spectacle.
5. Desktop and mobile are completed together.
6. Every screen must remain premium in loading, empty, partial-data, degraded, unauthorized, and error states.

## Shared Foundation Tokens

### Typography Roles

- Display Serif: high-authority page and index titles.
- Premium Sans: navigation, data labels, controls, tables, and body copy.
- Numeric Tabular: index levels, prices, returns, confidence, and timestamps.

### Spacing Scale

Use an 8-point base system with premium whitespace expansion at 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, and 128 pixels.

### Radius

- Data controls: 8-12px.
- Intelligence cards: 16-20px.
- Hero or flagship cards: 24px maximum.
- Avoid excessive pill-shaped containers.

### Motion

- Micro-state: 120-180ms.
- Card and panel transition: 180-240ms.
- Page reveal: under 320ms.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Trust Surface

Every material intelligence component must expose:

- updated_at
- source_coverage
- confidence_grade
- methodology_version
- evidence_link
- rights_status when relevant

## Kidults Visual Language

### Palette

- Deep Navy foundation.
- Controlled metallic gold accent.
- Archival charcoal and graphite surfaces.
- Limited use of vivid category color only for semantic differentiation.

### Character

- Precise, collectible, scarce, energetic, premium.
- Avoid toy-store visual language.
- Avoid gamer neon, excessive gradients, and retail marketplace density.

### Hero Modules

- Kidult 100
- Market Pulse
- Brand Momentum
- Category Rotation
- Collector Demand

## Artfund Visual Language

### Palette

- Ivory and warm white foundation.
- Charcoal and gallery black typography.
- Restrained antique gold or bronze accent.
- Artwork color must remain visually dominant.

### Character

- Museum-grade, institutional, calm, evidence-led, private-bank quality.
- Avoid decorative auction-house imitation.
- Avoid excessive black-gold luxury clichés.

### Hero Modules

- Global Art Market Index
- Artist Momentum
- Auction Liquidity
- Provenance Strength
- Market Segment Rotation

## Shared Page Anatomy

1. Brand and role-aware navigation.
2. Page kicker and authoritative title.
3. Executive interpretation.
4. Primary index or decision module.
5. Supporting analysis.
6. Evidence and methodology drawer.
7. Related reports, alerts, and export actions.

## Accessibility and International Readiness

- WCAG AA contrast minimum.
- Keyboard navigation for all actions.
- Visible focus state.
- Charts require text summaries.
- Dates, currencies, and numbers must be locale-aware.
- No critical state may depend on color alone.

## Release Gate

A portal page cannot be released unless:

- Luxury Brand Fit >= 95.
- Product Quality Score >= 90.
- Data Trust Score >= 90.
- Desktop and mobile acceptance criteria pass.
- Trust Surface is visible.
- Empty, loading, partial, degraded, unauthorized, and error states are designed.
