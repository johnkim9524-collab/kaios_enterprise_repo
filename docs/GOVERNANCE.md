# KIDULTS Governance Guide

## Governing authority

The [KIDULTS Platform Constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md) is the permanent human-readable authority for platform governance. Governance policies, registries, receipts, and machine contracts are subordinate implementations and may be stricter, but may not weaken, duplicate, or override constitutional authority.

Every governance gate must define its purpose, owner, recovery path, timeout, escalation, audit receipt, and exit condition. Uncertainty remains `HOLD`; authority must be explicit; evidence remains append-only; and no workflow, agent, repository, vendor, or infrastructure component may create implicit promotion or duplicate authority.

Detailed governed controls are maintained under `coordination/kidults/governance/`, `coordination/kidults/kpmo/`, `.github/`, and `docs/operations/`.
