## Product Readiness Interpretation

### Strong foundation

- Enterprise-oriented portal design
- Kidult 100 preview
- Brand and category intelligence
- Liquidity signals
- Trust surface
- Methodology and rights indicators

### Launch-critical gaps

- No Monthly Intelligence
- No Archive
- No subscriber or inquiry path
- No verified live-data Kidult 100
- No measured mobile, performance or accessibility evidence
- No paid product flow
