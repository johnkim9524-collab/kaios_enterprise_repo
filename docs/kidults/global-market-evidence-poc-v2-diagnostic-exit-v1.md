# KIDULTS Global Market Evidence PoC v2 — Diagnostic Exit v1

## Decision
PoC v2 is closed as a diagnostic cycle before a forced 320-product execution. Its primary purpose was to find structural weaknesses. The challenger sample itself exposed material P0/P1 defects, so multiplying the sample would have amplified known errors rather than created better evidence.

## Empirical results
Public Attention: 64 anchors, 60 mapped, 59 with 300+ days of history, zero transport errors. Attention is a strong self-collected component for Cultural Momentum / Emerging signals, never Demand, Liquidity or Transaction.

Regional Public Representation: 64 anchors, 60 mapped, 55 with multi-region/language representation, 31/32 Scopes covered, zero errors. This proves broad self-collectability of public regional representation, not regional market demand. Regional Market Significance still requires independent source families plus institution/release/venue evidence.

Challenger terminalization: 160/160 slots received explicit fail-closed states. 64 are PARTIAL_EVIDENCE, 32 BLOCKED_BY_REGIONAL_INDEPENDENCE, 64 BLOCKED_BY_MARKET_ACTIVITY, zero SELECTED. Therefore full 320 execution remains blocked by design.

## Main defects found and corrected
Generic discovery grammar was replaced by Scope-specific identity grammar. 160 direct provider probes were reduced to bounded Scope probes with retry/backoff. Attention transport was repaired. Regional mapping was changed from direct Wikidata entity search to Wikipedia title + langlinks. Scarcity, availability, activity, sold transaction, illiquidity and liquidity are now independent evidence classes. Provider necessity is no longer treated as a platform-wide assumption.

## Remaining structural gaps
The largest remaining P0 is Market Activity depth: rights-safe institutional channels provide strong Catalog/Provenance/Culture evidence but do not currently provide transaction/activity evidence. A second P0 is Regional Market Independence: public multi-language representation is broad, but regional demand/liquidity still needs independent venue/release/institution evidence.

## Provider conclusion
KIDULTS does not require a Provider as a platform foundation. Identity, Catalog, much Provenance/Culture, Public Attention and Regional Public Representation can remain provider-independent. Verified Sold History and Professional Authentication/Condition are Scope-specific capability problems. BrickLink demonstrates that even sold-history can sometimes be self-collected through an authenticated official API, so external provider necessity must be evaluated per Scope × Evidence Class × Rights × Decision Utility. Broad provider contact is not recommended.

## 8 Core Category conclusion
No canonical 8-category change is justified from this PoC. Market-activity coverage remains uneven; changing taxonomy now risks converting source-coverage bias into category strategy. Keep the 8 Core baseline, use dynamic weights and Dynamic Verticals, and revisit taxonomy only after persistent multi-period market evidence.

## Next cycle
PoC v2.1 should remediate the two open structural gaps before scale-up: regional independent evidence and market-activity adapters. First test BrickLink credentials/terms in Construction & Mechanical Sets and add regional institution/release/venue adapters. Re-terminalize challengers from actual role evidence, run targeted stress cases, and only then expand toward the full 320 diagnostic. Provider contact and Production remain HOLD.
