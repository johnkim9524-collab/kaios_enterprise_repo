# KIDULTS PoC v2 Diagnostic Remediation Design v1

## Decision
PoC v2 is treated as a defect-finding diagnostic, not a completion contest. The full 320-product run remains blocked until challenger-role evidence is defensible. The PoC findings are converted into canonical operating rules immediately.

## Empirical findings
- Public Attention: 60/64 mapped; 59/64 with 300d+ history; 0 request errors.
- Regional public representation: 60/64 mapped; 55/64 multi-region; 31/32 Scope coverage; 0 request errors.
- Challenger terminalization: 160/160 terminal; 64 PARTIAL_EVIDENCE; 32 BLOCKED_BY_REGIONAL_INDEPENDENCE; 64 BLOCKED_BY_MARKET_ACTIVITY; 0 SELECTED.

## Canonical remediation
### Regional depth
Regional is split into three distinct layers: Regional Public Representation, Regional Market Significance, and Regional Liquidity. Public language/region representation cannot prove market significance. Market significance requires an independent source family plus institution/release/venue evidence. Liquidity requires activity or transaction evidence.

### Scarcity and liquidity
Supply Scarcity, Tradable Availability, Observed Market Activity, Verified Transaction Activity, Liquidity, Failed-sale Rate and Time-to-Sale are separate evidence/metric families. Missing data is not illiquidity; listings are not sold transactions; price is not scarcity.

### Dynamic Vertical promotion
Attention is a nomination and Cultural Momentum component only. A Dynamic Vertical may not be promoted from attention alone. Promotion requires independent market-activity evidence, Track B validation and KPMO governance.

### Provider strategy
Provider necessity is never platform-wide by default. It is assessed per Scope × Evidence Class × Rights × Decision Utility. Identity/catalog/culture, public attention and regional public representation remain provider-independent foundation capabilities. External licensing is justified only for residual capability gaps that materially improve customer decisions and pass rights/dependency review.

### Cross-Scope normalization
Every cross-Scope score must include Normalization Confidence. Uneven market-activity depth across Scopes automatically lowers normalization confidence and may block ranking/public comparison.

### PoC scale gate
Known P0 defects block scale-up. Unresolved challenger slots remain fail-closed and may not be fabricated to satisfy a target sample size. The next cycle is PoC v2.1, focused only on Regional Market Independence and Scope-specific Market Activity adapters.

## Track A–E alignment
Track A collects and separates evidence classes; Track B independently validates role evidence and Provider necessity; Track C renders projection/confidence only; Track D operates bounded adapters and revalidation; Track E surfaces defects/gates/decisions. No Track may create missing market truth.
