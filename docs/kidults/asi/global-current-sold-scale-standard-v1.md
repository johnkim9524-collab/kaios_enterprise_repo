# KIDULTS Global Current-SOLD Scale Standard v1

**Owner:** KPMO

**Status:** Mandatory / fail-closed

**Effective:** 2026-08-30

KIDULTS is a global intelligence platform, not a single-market pricing pilot. A total record count can prove ingestion mechanics, but it cannot prove global product readiness. Every market stage therefore requires simultaneous transaction-volume, eight-vertical, source-owner, region, currency, comparable-object and freshness coverage.

| Stage | Rights-clear Current-SOLD | Per active vertical | Active verticals | Independent owners / vertical | Regions / vertical | Claim ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| E2E empirical admission | 120 | — | 1 | 1 | 1 | Pipeline validated only |
| Global market intelligence beta | 40,000 | 5,000 | 8 | 4 | 3 | Bounded beta evidence only |
| Global commercial launch evidence-ready | 400,000 | 50,000 | 8 | 5 | 5 | Evidence-ready, not authorized |
| Global normal operations evidence-ready | 2,000,000 | 250,000 | 8 | 8 | 6 | Evidence-ready, not authorized |
| Global leadership evidence-ready | 10,000,000 | 1,000,000 | 8 | 12 | 8 | Leadership evidence-ready, not authorized |

There is no fixed maximum. Scale continues only where exact-purpose rights, quality, customer utility, retention, privacy and unit economics remain acceptable.

## Non-negotiable claim boundary

- `100` or `120` events are an empirical admission batch, never a launch population.
- Reference facts, asking prices, listings and synthetic fixtures never count as Current-SOLD.
- One transaction never establishes current price.
- Stage thresholds do not grant Public, paid, Production or G5 authority.
- Projection remains downstream of immutable Evidence and independent Track B review.

## Public posture

A rights-cleared Reference/Research surface may be evaluated under its own release gate. Market-price, liquidity, projection and paid-intelligence claims remain fail-closed until the applicable evidence stage and separate authority gate both pass.
