# KIDULTS Cross-Scope Meta Intelligence Design v1

## Why this layer exists
Scope depth answers what an object/market is, why it is valuable and why it moves. Meta Intelligence answers how wrong we may be, what can go wrong, whether signals may be distorted, whether sources are resilient, whether cross-category comparison is valid, and whether an output improves a customer decision.

## Eight meta layers
1. Downside / Risk Intelligence — risk profile, never unsupported buy/sell advice.
2. Confidence Decay / Evidence Aging — VERIFIED → STALE → REVALIDATION_REQUIRED → NOT_VERIFIED.
3. Market Integrity / Manipulation Detection — anomaly detection only; anomaly is never automatically a fraud allegation.
4. Decision Utility — BUY/HOLD/SELL/COMPARE/AUTHENTICATE/INSURE/CONSIGN/WATCH decision relevance, confidence improvement, time saved and error reduction.
5. Source Resilience / Substitutability — source-family independence, removal stability, fallback and rights-revocation resilience.
6. Cross-Scope Normalization Integrity — every normalized score must carry Normalization Confidence and bias limitations.
7. Product / Cluster Lifecycle — EMERGING, ACCELERATING, MATURE, OVERHEATED, COOLING, DECLINING, REDISCOVERY; distinct from overall Market Regime.
8. Counterfactual / Simulation — bounded scenarios such as grade, condition, event, region, liquidity or attention changes; simulation is never observed truth.

## Platform rule
This is a meta layer above Scope-specific Object/Market Intelligence, not eight more mandatory data fields in every Scope. Universal layers apply across all Scopes; Counterfactual Simulation is enabled only where evidence and methodology support it.

## Truth guards
Evidence Before Metrics; missing is not zero; attention is not demand or transaction; anomaly is not fraud; simulation is not observation; normalized scores require normalization confidence; Provider is Evidence Source, never Truth.

## Track alignment
Track A produces evidence-backed candidates. Track B independently validates. Track C consumes projection only and exposes confidence/limitations. Track D runs aging/revalidation/anomaly operations without generating business truth. Track E surfaces decision/risk/drift/simulation state without generating market truth.
