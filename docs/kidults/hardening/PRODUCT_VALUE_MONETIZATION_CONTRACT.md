# KIDULTS Product Value & Monetization Contract

Status: internal product/commercial design baseline. Customer willingness-to-pay and commercial economics remain unvalidated until supported by external evidence.

## Product decision contract

Every product must identify the customer decision it improves, the evidence used, freshness requirement, confidence/provenance, and what action becomes better or faster.

| Product | Primary decision improved |
|---|---|
| Kidult 100 | Which collectible assets/categories deserve attention now and why |
| Category Intelligence | Which categories are strengthening, weakening or rotating |
| Valuation / Comparables | What value range is defensible from traceable comparables |
| Market Momentum / Liquidity | Whether demand and transactionability are improving or deteriorating |
| Scarcity Intelligence | Whether supply constraints are genuine, persistent and value-relevant |
| Collector Sentiment | Whether cultural/collector attention is accelerating or fading |
| Brand / Franchise Intelligence | Which brands/franchises have durable or improving canon strength |
| Monthly Intelligence Report | What changed materially during the month and what decisions follow |
| Historical Archive | How the current state compares with prior regimes and cycles |
| Alerts / Watchlists | Which monitored entity has crossed a decision-relevant threshold |

Features without a clear decision, measurable value or moat should be removed or deprioritized.

## Packaging baseline

- **Free**: limited delayed discovery and public benchmark context.
- **Premium**: deeper rankings, movement explanations, alerts and category context.
- **Professional**: valuation/comparables, advanced history, exports and professional workflows.
- **Enterprise**: governed teams, higher coverage/freshness, audit/provenance, custom feeds and support.
- **API**: machine-readable licensed intelligence subject to entitlement and provider rights.

## Pricing logic

Pricing should scale with decision value, freshness, coverage, historical depth, exclusivity, workflow integration and service obligations—not raw record count alone.

## Unit-economics contract

Track revenue and gross value contribution against provider cost, compute/storage cost, support cost, validation cost and human intervention. Minimum required measurements include cost per validated object, cost per intelligence output, human minutes per 1,000 records and intelligence value per unit cost.

## KPI contract

Retention, active use, decision completion, alert engagement, data freshness, provenance coverage, accuracy/manual correction, paid conversion, expansion, API consumption and enterprise use.

## Entitlement controls

Commercial tier, data rights, geography, redistribution, export/API scope, historical depth and freshness must be entitlement-controlled. Unknown provider rights must fail closed for commercial delivery.

## External evidence required

Customer interviews/behavior, willingness-to-pay, paid conversion, retention, realized decision value, provider commercial rights, support burden and measured product-level unit economics.
