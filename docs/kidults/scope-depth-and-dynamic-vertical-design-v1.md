# KIDULTS Scope Depth + Dynamic Vertical Design v1

## Decision
The 32-Scope baseline remains the current canonical operating taxonomy, but Scope depth is expanded and market change is absorbed first through Dynamic Verticals rather than premature taxonomy edits.

## Depth model
Every Scope must support, where applicable: Market Regime; Generational Canon; Cultural Momentum; Authentication/Condition Risk; Condition-adjusted Comparables; Regional Liquidity; Ownership Friction.

### Truth separation
Attention is not Demand. Demand is not Transaction. Asking price is not Sold price. Public verification is not bulk commercial reuse authorization. All metrics remain Evidence Before Metrics and NOT_VERIFIED when evidence is insufficient.

## Dynamic Vertical
A Dynamic Vertical is an evidence-backed market/customer cluster below a Canonical Scope. It can change faster than Scope taxonomy and can drive Representative Product, Market Cell, Evidence Demand and Portal prioritization without rewriting canonical history.

Lifecycle: SIGNAL → WATCH → EMERGING → VALIDATED_VERTICAL → SCOPE_CANDIDATE → PROMOTED_OR_ARCHIVED.

Promotion requires distinct customer decision, identity grammar, condition/provenance logic, market behavior, intelligence output, persistent multi-period signal, geographic significance, Track B PASS and KPMO approval. No autonomous promotion.

## Initial watchlist
Digital-Physical Collectibles; Contemporary Character Collectibles; 1990s/2000s Nostalgia Assets; Independent Watchmaking; Japanese Modern Classics; Archival Fashion; Vintage Computing/Early Technology; Digital Watches.

These are WATCH candidates, not claims that the market has already justified new Canonical Scopes.

## 8 Core Category governance
The eight Core Categories remain a stable baseline, but their weights are dynamic. Priority is recalculated from structural market depth, market momentum, liquidity depth, global breadth, customer attention and evidence coverage. Category creation/removal requires persistent evidence and Scope Drift review; one market-data wave cannot change taxonomy.

## Platform effect
Track A collects and interprets signals but cannot promote taxonomy. Track B independently validates drift and promotion. Track C may expose Dynamic Verticals as projections without presenting them as Canonical Scope. Track D sizes runtime from Product × Market Cell × Evidence Demand × cadence. Track E surfaces watch/emerging/promotion decisions and blockers. Provider contact remains HOLD until Global Market Evidence PoC v2 quantifies remaining capability gaps.
