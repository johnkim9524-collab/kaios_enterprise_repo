# KIDULTS Operations Support Guide

Support may observe and route incidents but may not alter rights, evidence, canonical identity, Confidence, promotion or release authority.

## Detection
Capture user-visible symptom, route, object ID, request ID, time and synthetic label without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Isolation
Remove no evidence. Route provider, rights, canonical, runtime and portal symptoms to their owning operational lane and keep actions blocked.

## Recovery
Provide safe refresh or last-verified-view guidance only. Engineering recovery follows the incident and recovery guides.

## Verification
Confirm the incident receipt, corrected state, accessibility, portal isolation and absence of unauthorized data exposure before closure.

## Communication
Use plain language, disclose uncertainty and HOLD state, and never claim market accuracy, provider authority or restoration before exact evidence exists.
