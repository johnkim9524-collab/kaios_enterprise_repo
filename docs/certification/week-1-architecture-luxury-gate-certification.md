## Certification Decision

**PASS — Week 2 staging implementation is authorized.**

The certification authorizes implementation of the shared registries, canonical database, evidence ledger, methodology registry, staging APIs, and first premium portal shells.

## Conditions of Authorization

1. Kidults production remains protected and unchanged unless a separate promotion gate is passed.
2. Artfund implementation begins in staging and must not claim production certification.
3. New sources must pass rights classification before collection or display.
4. Every material intelligence response must expose freshness, confidence, methodology, evidence, and rights status.
5. Desktop and mobile responsive delivery must be implemented together.
6. Loading, empty, partial, degraded, unauthorized, rights-restricted, and error states are mandatory.
7. Destructive migrations require backup, rollback, and rehearsal evidence.
