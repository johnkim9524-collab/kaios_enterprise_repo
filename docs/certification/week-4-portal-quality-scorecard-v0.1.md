## Mandatory Pass Conditions

- Trust Surface is visible on every material score and index.
- No unexplained score, index, or recommendation.
- Loading, empty, partial, degraded, unauthorized, restricted, and error states are implemented.
- Artfund supports an explicit provenance-disputed state.
- 320 px minimum viewport has no horizontal overflow.
- All primary actions have at least 44 px touch targets.
- CSV and JSON exports are deterministic.
- PDF export contract requires methodology, evidence, and rights appendices.
- Viewer export is blocked.
- Rights, confidence, evidence, methodology, freshness, and provenance gates fail closed.

## Beta Certification Rule

The Week 4 Portal MVP can be certified only when both verticals pass every mandatory condition and meet all weighted thresholds. A high aggregate score cannot compensate for a failed rights, evidence, methodology, security, or mobile gate.
