# A13-B22 Outreach Dispatch Ledger & Evidence Audit

## Objective
Provide a deterministic, secret-safe and auditable command-line workflow for recording provider outreach, responses and verified evidence without storing personal contact details.

## Scope
- contacted/responded/diligence/rejected status updates
- append-only dispatch and response ledger
- duplicate dispatch protection
- evidence validation for eight required evidence classes
- role-level pilot-ready calculation
- machine-readable audit report

## Safety
- staging only
- production remains untouched
- no secrets, recipient addresses or personal contact details are stored
- unknown values remain unknown
- production promotion remains blocked by default
- mobile gates remain required at 320px, 360px, 390px and 430px
