# A13-B18 Provider Acquisition & Live Pilot Readiness

## Objective
Prepare the commercial, legal and technical handoff required to select approved external providers and run a controlled live-data pilot.

## Integrated Scope
- provider evaluation matrix for transactions, supply and cultural demand
- commercial, technical and rights scoring
- pilot manifest and approval gates
- onboarding checklist and handoff runbook
- machine-readable live-pilot readiness report

## Safety
- Production remains untouched.
- No provider is approved without explicit rights, endpoint, health, credential and commercial review.
- No secret value is committed, printed or rendered.
- Illustrative fallback remains available.
- Production promotion remains blocked by default.

## Completion Gates
- one preferred provider candidate per required role
- independent provider-family minimum preserved
- contractual production and retention rights approved
- pilot budget and quota accepted
- health and latency thresholds accepted
- live pilot manifest complete
- rollback and fallback verified
- mobile validation at 320px, 360px, 390px and 430px
