# A13-B20 Provider Outreach & Due Diligence Execution

## Objective
Convert the verified B19 candidate shortlist into an executable outreach and diligence workflow.

## Scope
- role-based outreach queue
- contact and response-state tracking
- commercial, technical and rights diligence questions
- evidence registry
- provider scorecard
- pilot-approval gate
- machine-readable outreach status

## Safety
- staging only
- no secret values or personal contact details committed
- unknown facts remain unknown
- no candidate is pilot-approved without recorded evidence
- production promotion remains blocked by default
- mobile gates remain 320px, 360px, 390px and 430px
