Methodology

How evidence becomes intelligence.

Kidults documents source standards, scoring logic, confidence thresholds, revision history and the limits of each conclusion.

Framework

Five stages protect interpretability.

Collection, verification, normalization, scoring and review are separated so users can understand how a conclusion was formed.

01

Collect

Gather structured market, release, resale and cultural evidence.

02

Verify

Remove duplicate, stale or unsupported observations.

03

Interpret

Combine quantitative movement with evidence quality and cultural context.

Confidence

Every score carries a strength of evidence.

Confidence reflects source diversity, recency, consistency and coverage depth. It does not express certainty about future prices.

Limitations

Interpretation remains bounded.

Kidults does not present unsupported projections, guaranteed pricing outcomes or traffic-only popularity as market intelligence.

Revision history

Major changes remain visible.

Weighting, source and classification changes are recorded to protect historical comparability.

Transparency

Trust is part of the product.

Methodology exists so each benchmark, report and signal can be challenged and understood.
