Brand movement

Durability is concentrating.

Brands gaining momentum share three traits: identifiable visual language, disciplined release cadence and cross-generational recognition.

  • Legacy character properties gained repeat purchases.
  • Over-supplied collaborations lost secondary depth.
  • Provenance-rich editions held pricing better.
