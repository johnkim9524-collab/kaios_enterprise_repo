01 · Principles

Comparable, explainable and governed.

01

Evidence-led

No signal is promoted solely because it is popular. Source quality, freshness and corroboration determine whether it can influence a score.

02

Category-aware

Liquidity, cadence and price discovery differ by category. Comparisons are normalized before categories are ranked together.

03

Decision-ready

Every output pairs direction with confidence so decision-makers can distinguish a strong signal from a fragile observation.

02 · Composite score

Four dimensions form the K100 score.

The staging methodology uses a 100-point composite. Weights express the present research design and remain subject to certification before production use.

DimensionWeightWhat it measures
Market momentum30%Direction and persistence of demand across approved signals.
Liquidity30%Transaction depth, price discovery and ability to realize value.
Brand strength25%Recognition, durability, community reach and cultural relevance.
Evidence quality15%Source rights, coverage, corroboration, freshness and completeness.

Interpretation: A higher score indicates stronger relative decision evidence within the covered universe. It is not a price target, valuation or investment recommendation.

03 · Confidence

Confidence is separate from direction.

A positive signal may still carry low confidence. Kidults therefore assigns an evidence grade independently of the composite score.

A

Decision-grade

Multiple approved sources, recent evidence and strong corroboration.

B

Supported

Adequate coverage with limited gaps that do not reverse the signal.

C

Directional

Useful for monitoring, but material coverage or freshness gaps remain.

D

Insufficient

Not suitable for external decision use; held behind the evidence gate.

04 · Evidence lifecycle

Every signal must pass the release gate.

  1. CollectAcquire permitted public, partner or proprietary evidence.
  2. NormalizeResolve units, category definitions, time windows and duplicates.
  3. ValidateCheck rights, freshness, completeness, anomalies and corroboration.
  4. ScoreCalculate normalized dimensions and attach confidence metadata.
  5. ReleasePublish only when evidence and governance thresholds are satisfied.

05 · Limitations and responsible use

Intelligence supports judgment; it does not replace it.

Coverage is selective and may not represent the entire global collectibles market.

Secondary-market data can be fragmented, delayed or affected by thin trading.

Category scores are relative to the covered universe and methodology version.

Staging values are illustrative until source rights and production certification are complete.

Kidults intelligence is not financial, legal, tax or investment advice.

Governance status

Transparent by design.

Material methodology changes will receive a new version, effective date and documented rationale. Historical reports retain the version used at publication.
