Methodology

How evidence becomes intelligence.

Kidults combines market behaviour, cultural durability, geographic breadth and source confidence through a versioned, inspectable methodology.

Scoring model

Five dimensions. One accountable benchmark.

No single price spike or social signal can determine a score.

M

Momentum

Direction, persistence and breadth of observable market movement.

L

Liquidity

Turnover quality, bid depth, spread and repeat participation.

D

Durability

Longevity, recognisability, ritual and cultural persistence.

G

Geography

Regional breadth and consistency across independent markets.

C

Confidence

Source diversity, freshness, density and agreement.

V

Versioning

Documented revisions, thresholds and governance history.

Confidence engine

Every score carries an evidence weight.

Confidence rises when independent, fresh and diverse sources agree. It falls when evidence is narrow, stale or contradictory.

High confidence

Decision-ready evidence with broad source agreement.

Moderate confidence

Useful for monitoring, with some limitations.

Research-only signals that require confirmation.
