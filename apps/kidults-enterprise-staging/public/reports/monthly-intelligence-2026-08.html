Executive summary

Momentum is concentrated, not broad.

Premium categories with recognizable creative identity and active secondary markets are carrying the strongest current signals. The near-term opportunity lies in selectivity: high-confidence categories are separating from fragmented long-tail assets.

01 · Market pulse

Demand favors established signals.

Demand remains concentrated in premium categories with strong brand recognition and active secondary markets. In the staging dataset, conviction is strongest where repeat transactions, global recognition and visible collector communities overlap.

02 · Category intelligence

Designer toys lead the current ranking.

Designer toys lead 30-day momentum at 5.8%, followed by trading cards at 3.1% and action figures at 1.7%. These values are illustrative staging signals and must not be interpreted as live investment performance.

03 · Liquidity

Depth remains uneven.

High-confidence categories show healthier turnover and clearer price discovery. Long-tail assets remain more fragmented, increasing execution risk and widening the difference between listed value and realized value.
