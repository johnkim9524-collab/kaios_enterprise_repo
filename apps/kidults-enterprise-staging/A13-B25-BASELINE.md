# A13-B25 Staging Release Freeze

## Objective
Freeze the approved Kidults staging product after B24 and publish a deterministic executive handoff package.

## Frozen scope
- B10-B24 staging product and UI
- data adapter, fallback, freshness and provenance
- source registry, resilience and operations certification
- scoring, monthly intelligence and archive outputs
- provider acquisition, outreach, response intake and audit ledgers
- manual dispatch packets and operator confirmation gates

## External technical dependencies
- provider outreach must be sent manually
- provider responses and commercial terms remain external
- provider production-use and retention rights remain unapproved
- production credentials, rights and endpoints remain unavailable
- live-pilot approval remains pending

## Separate executive decisions
Corporate formation and trademark filing are managed outside this technical release freeze. They are not B25 engineering blockers and are not included in the release gate.

## Safety
- staging only
- production untouched
- production promotion remains blocked by default
- no secrets or personal contact details are committed
- no provider is marked contacted without a real dispatch confirmation

## Release rule
No additional staging feature expansion after B25 unless it fixes a verified defect, closes a confirmed compliance gap, or is required by an approved live-pilot provider.
