# A19 — Data Coverage & Productization Gap Matrix

## Objective
Convert A18 scale evidence into an explicit build-vs-buy data strategy before provider outreach.

## Decision model
Every intelligence dimension is classified as SELF-FIRST, HYBRID, or PROVIDER-REQUIRED. Provider contact is downstream of this classification, never a substitute for it.

## Self-first candidates
Identity/canon, market observation, availability, culture/attention, and macro/category signals should be built and product-tested internally first.

## Hybrid / provider gaps
Transaction pricing requires authoritative sold data. Auction/private-sale intelligence requires venue outcomes. Provenance requires defensible event history. Authentication/condition requires trusted specialist observations. These gaps become provider requirements rather than broad requests for data.

## Productization contract
Each product must identify its source dimension, data strategy, readiness, provenance, freshness and publication eligibility. Provider-required products remain dependency-blocked until licensed data satisfies policy.

## Provider outreach contract
Requests must specify required fields, stable identifiers, provenance, freshness, usage rights and incremental delivery. Commercial outreach starts only after internal collection demonstrates the exact missing signal.

## Safety
A19 performs classification and certification only. It does not contact providers, publish production intelligence, embed credentials, or mutate external systems.
