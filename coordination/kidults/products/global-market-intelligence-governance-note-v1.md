# Market Intelligence Governance Note

The product family is a derived intelligence layer over governed evidence and approved regional baseline snapshots. It is not a redistribution channel for provider data and it is not permitted to infer factual regional market share from collection allocation, source volume or bootstrap priors.

All customer-visible claims remain bounded by geography, confidence, freshness, evidence coverage and rights. Global claims fail closed when representation is insufficient. Public and Production release remain HOLD until the release gates pass.
