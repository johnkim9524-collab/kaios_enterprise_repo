# Global Collectibles Market Intelligence v1

This product family converts approved regional market evidence into customer-facing intelligence without exposing raw provider data or confusing collection volume with market size.

## Products
- Global Collectibles Market Map
- Regional Market Pulse
- Cross-Border Opportunity Map
- Category Regional Scorecard
- Market Structure Report

## Decision value
Customers can understand where collectible markets are large, mature, active, liquid, accelerating, weakening or under-observed, with geography, freshness, confidence and limitations visible.

## Truth boundary
Only approved Projection is customer-facing. Bootstrap allocation is never represented as market share. Unknown is never converted to zero. Global claims remain fail-closed until geography and evidence gates pass. Cross-border opportunities require condition, tax, fee, FX and time comparability.

## Release state
Pre-publication product foundation only. Public release and Production remain HOLD until empirical regional baseline, independent Track B assessment, redistribution rights, Projection, Portal QA and runtime gates pass.
