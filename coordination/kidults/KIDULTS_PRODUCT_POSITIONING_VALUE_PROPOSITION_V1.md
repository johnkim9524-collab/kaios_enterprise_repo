## 1. Executive decision

KIDULTS is positioned as a **governed collectibles intelligence platform**.

> **KIDULTS turns fragmented object, evidence and market observations into governed, explainable intelligence for collectors and institutions.**

KIDULTS is not a marketplace, a price-only database, a grading company, an opaque AI recommendation engine or a synthetic ranking surface. It is the decision infrastructure between fragmented evidence and a defensible action.

Its category-defining advantage is the integration of:

1. canonical object identity;
2. evidence and provenance;
3. rights and freshness controls;
4. market observations and comparables;
5. confidence and rankability;
6. governed Projections;
7. audience-specific decision lenses; and
8. immutable decision and audit history.

The commercial promise is therefore not “more data.” It is:

> **Know what is known, why it is credible, what remains closed, and what to do next.**

## 2. Market problem

Collectibles markets are structurally difficult to analyze:

- the same object is described differently across sources;
- transaction evidence is sparse, delayed and rights-constrained;
- provenance, condition and variant differences change comparability;
- price without venue, date, currency and observation type is misleading;
- market liquidity and scarcity are frequently asserted without adequate evidence;
- generated editorial imagery can be confused with market evidence;
- institutions need coverage, concentration and auditability while collectors need object-level clarity and possible next actions;
- data-provider dependence can transfer the product's system of record outside KIDULTS.

Most products solve only one layer: listing, auction archive, grading, image recognition, valuation or portfolio tracking. KIDULTS is designed to govern the full path from observation to decision while failing closed when evidence is insufficient.

## 5. Product innovation

KIDULTS innovation is not one model or interface feature. It is a system-level design that changes how collectible intelligence is admitted, explained and consumed.

### 5.1 Evidence Before Metrics

No metric is stronger than its evidence. Missing is never silently converted to zero. When the evidence, rights, freshness or rankability gate is closed, the product shows the reason, the opening condition and the available user action.

### 5.2 Projection as a governed product layer

The customer interface does not read arbitrary raw data. An approved Projection packages:

- scope and universe;
- observation period;
- geography, venue and currency;
- source count and independence;
- transformation and methodology versions;
- confidence and rankability;
- freshness and update timestamp;
- missing-data treatment;
- rights and limitations;
- Projection ID and audit lineage.

This makes provider changes possible without changing the product's public analytical grammar.

### 5.3 Object–Evidence–Market integration

A collectible is not treated as a ticker. Identity, maker, model, variant, year, provenance, specification, condition, comparables, liquidity, scarcity, cultural significance, risks, evidence, rights and audit history are modeled as one governed dossier.

### 5.4 Explainable comparable distance

Comparable selection is a disclosed analytical judgment, not a hidden similarity score. Every comparable must state which attributes increase or reduce relevance and which evidence is unavailable.

### 5.5 Confidence as an output

Confidence is visible at field, claim and Projection level. KIDULTS does not hide uncertainty behind a single polished number.

### 5.6 One evidence base, two decision lenses

The same governed evidence supports different decision grammars without creating contradictory truths:

- **Collector Lens:** What changed → Why it matters → Comparable context → Liquidity → Risk → Possible action
- **Institutional Lens:** Universe → Coverage → Market scale → Depth → Turnover → Concentration → Exposure → Confidence

### 5.7 Cross-vertical benchmark governance

Kidult 100 is designed as a rules-governed benchmark rather than an editorial ranking. Eligibility, evidence threshold, liquidity threshold, weighting, concentration caps, rebalance, event treatment, rights, method versioning and restatement policy precede publication.

## 6. Defensible moat

The durable advantage is the compound graph and governance layer:

`Object Graph + Evidence Graph + Market Observation Graph + Rights Graph + Confidence Engine + Projection Engine + Decision History`

Each additional admitted observation can improve identity resolution, comparable quality, confidence calibration and change detection. The defensibility comes from governed relationships and accumulated decision lineage, not from exclusive dependence on a single feed.

### Provider boundary

**KAIOS/KIDULTS Core owns:**

- source registry and collection framework;
- normalization and canonical entity resolution;
- KIDULTS domain ontology and mapping;
- evidence admission, independence, freshness and rights interpretation;
- scoring, rankability and confidence;
- derived analytics and comparable distance;
- Projection schema and generation;
- provider switching;
- reporting, audit, observability, rollback and release governance.

**External providers may supply:**

- authorized source observations;
- transaction or listing feeds;
- licensed images;
- grading or condition observations;
- specialist assessments where explicitly required.

**Operating rule:** no provider becomes the system of record for KIDULTS intelligence. Procurement remains gap-driven and evidence-led: proof before procurement.

- governed intelligence for illiquid, fragmented collectible markets;
- object-level and market-level analysis in one system;
- explicit about evidence, rights, freshness, confidence and limitations;
- designed for provider switching and auditability;

- a marketplace or dealer;
- a price guarantee or investment recommendation service;
- a grading authority unless separately authorized;

## 11. Acceptance criteria for productization

A product is ready to move from candidate to externally claimable only when:

- its target user, job, outcome and exclusion boundary are explicit;
- the required Projection fields map 1:1 to the product interface or API;
- every empty state states current condition, closure reason, opening requirement and available action;
- object and claim states support VERIFIED, INFERRED, UNAVAILABLE, RIGHTS BLOCKED and STALE;
- confidence and rankability are not conflated;
- generated/editorial visuals are visibly separated from market evidence;
- all interactive controls have a valid destination, action or disabled reason;
- mobile information priority is verified at 390, 430 and 768 px;
- every analytical surface can open its provenance and Governance record;
- provider replacement does not change the external analytical contract;
- the immutable Candidate/Evidence → validation → Projection → release chain is complete;
- Production/Public promotion has explicit G5 approval.
