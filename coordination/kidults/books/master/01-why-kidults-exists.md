## 1. The purpose

KIDULTS exists to **make the invisible visible**.

Collectible objects connect culture with markets, but the information required to understand them is fragmented across catalogues, listings, archives, graders, specialists, owners, images and transactions. Identity may be ambiguous. Condition and provenance may be incomplete. A visible price may not be a sale. A confident statement may still be unrankable. A missing observation may be mistakenly read as zero.

KIDULTS turns those fragments into governed, explainable intelligence for collectors and institutions. Its task is not to decorate uncertainty with a number. Its task is to show:

- what is known;
- which evidence supports it;
- how current and usable that evidence is;
- what remains unavailable, inferred, stale or rights-blocked;
- why a conclusion is or is not rankable; and
- what the user can responsibly do next.

The governing promise is concise:

> Know the object. Understand the evidence. See the decision boundary.

## 2. The market problem

Collectibles do not behave like a clean securities market. The same object may appear under different names, variants or identifiers. Venue, currency, date, condition, provenance and rights alter the meaning of an observation. Sparse transactions and inconsistent disclosure weaken naive comparisons. A large record count can coexist with poor evidence quality.

This creates two customer problems.

For collectors, the question is practical: *What is this object, what changed, what is comparable, what should I verify, and what risk remains?*

For institutions, the question is systemic: *What universe is covered, how deep and concentrated is the evidence, how reproducible is the method, and can every conclusion be audited?*

KIDULTS serves both without creating two truths. One governed evidence base supports two decision grammars:

- **Collector Lens:** What changed → Why it matters → Comparable context → Liquidity → Risk → Possible action.
- **Institutional Lens:** Universe → Coverage → Market scale → Depth → Turnover → Concentration → Exposure → Confidence.

## 3. The product position

KIDULTS is a governed collectibles intelligence platform. It is not a marketplace, a price-only archive, a grading authority, an opaque recommendation engine or a synthetic ranking surface.

Its owned decision layer is:

`Observation → Canonical Object → Evidence Admission → Independent Validation → Governed Projection → Decision Lens → Audit History`

The flagship product sequence is dependency-led:

1. **KIDULTS Object Passport** establishes canonical identity, provenance, condition, rights and audit history.
2. **KIDULTS Market Projection API** carries only approved intelligence with lineage, confidence, freshness and limitations.
3. **Kidult 100 Index** becomes possible only after universe, eligibility, liquidity, weighting, concentration, rebalance, rights and restatement rules are empirically supportable.
4. **KIDULTS Intelligence, Reports and Enterprise** turn those foundations into research, monitoring, collaboration and decision workflows.

No candidate product is represented as launched before its evidence, rights, independent assessment, Projection, experience, runtime and approval gates pass.

## 4. The value created

KIDULTS does not compete for the largest pile of raw records. It competes to own the governed decision layer between fragmented observations and defensible action.

| Stakeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| Collector | Understand the object, comparable context, evidence limits, liquidity uncertainty, risk and the next responsible action |
| Institution | Define the universe, inspect coverage and concentration, reproduce methods and audit every released conclusion |
| Research partner | Pair claims with independent evidence, freshness, rights, transformations and limitations |
| Data/platform partner | Reuse approved intelligence without transferring KIDULTS identity, confidence, methodology or system-of-record control |
| Provider/specialist | Contribute bounded observations through explicit rights, retention, provenance and exit terms |

The durable moat is not a single model or exclusive feed. It is the compounding relationship among the Object Graph, Evidence Graph, Market Observation Graph, Rights Graph, confidence and rankability controls, Projection layer and decision history.

## 5. Portal doctrine

The Portal has three simultaneous responsibilities:

1. **Brand Surface** — express the seriousness, clarity and cultural range of KIDULTS.
2. **Product Interface** — let a user inspect, compare, filter, save, monitor and understand governed intelligence.
3. **Market Sensor** — reveal which questions, objects, filters, evidence paths and actions users actually need.

Every line, plane, word, font, sentence, message, number, graph and colour must improve explanation. The interface is successful only when it makes method, trust conditions and next action legible. Editorial imagery may establish context; it never becomes evidence.
