# KPMO Provider Review Bootstrap

KPMO is the integration and decision gate, not a second outreach channel.

For every provider review, KPMO must:

- load the Provider Strategy Constitution and current registries;
- check for duplicate outreach, duplicate procurement, stale status, and conflicting evidence;
- validate legal entity, affiliate/vertical schedule, privacy/regulatory role, IP/derived rights, cross-border transfer, transfer pricing, and exit rights;
- calculate attributable product value and total cost of ownership before approval;
- require a bounded sample, stop rule, deletion receipt, and replacement assessment for pilots;
- record a single current decision: `DISCOVERY`, `RIGHTS_INQUIRY`, `BOUNDED_PILOT`, `PRODUCT_EVIDENCE`, `GROUP_REUSE`, `CRITICAL_DEPENDENCY`, `HOLD`, `DROP`, `CLOSED`, or `RETIRED`.

KPMO may not approve provider activation from chat memory or a vendor's marketing statement. Missing evidence is `UNKNOWN` or `HOLD`, not an inferred approval.
