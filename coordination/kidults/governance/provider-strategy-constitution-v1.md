# Provider Strategy Constitution v1

**Authority:** Intelligence Holdings / KPMO  
**Status:** MANDATORY / FAIL-CLOSED  
**Scope:** Track Z, KPMO, and every provider-related agent or workflow

## 1. Default operating model

Intelligence Holdings (IH) centrally orchestrates provider procurement, identity, rights, evidence provenance, retention, deletion, security, and replacement. A vertical may consume only the purpose, region, fields, retention period, display scope, and derivative rights recorded in its approved vertical schedule.

Central procurement does not make IH and every vertical a single legal person, controller, or unrestricted licensee. Contracting entity, affiliate use, privacy role, regulated-data boundary, and tax treatment must be verified separately.

## 2. Non-negotiable rules

- No duplicate independent procurement by a vertical unless IH Legal/Data Governance approves an exception and the registry records rationale, cost, coverage, and exit plan.
- No provider activation, customer display, resale, model training, or raw retention without written rights evidence.
- Provider raw records, canonical identity, normalized facts, evidence receipts, derived metrics, models, and embeddings are distinct assets with distinct rights.
- `CLOSED`, `DROP`, `HOLD`, and `UNKNOWN` providers cannot be promoted by narrative, chat memory, or an unregistered configuration.
- Existing correspondence must be checked before outreach; sent messages must not be resent without an explicit new-contact decision.
- Production, Public, G5, contract, credential, and spend gates remain protected.

## 3. Required provider lifecycle

`DISCOVERY → RIGHTS_INQUIRY → BOUNDED_PILOT → PRODUCT_EVIDENCE → GROUP_REUSE → CRITICAL_DEPENDENCY`

Each transition requires a registry update, evidence reference, owner, cost exposure, stop rule, and replacement assessment. A provider can move to `HOLD`, `DROP`, `CLOSED`, or `RETIRED` at any stage.

## 4. Minimum rights record

Every provider record must state: legal provider and contracting entity; named IH entity; permitted verticals/affiliates; countries; purpose; fields; API/SaaS/display/resale/ML rights; attribution; TTL; backup deletion; correction; incident; audit; termination assistance; price and overage; data portability; and fallback provider.

## 5. Reporting contract

Track Z reports verified provider facts, rights gaps, cost/coverage, and negotiation actions. KPMO decides GO/HOLD/DROP/CLOSED and records the authority boundary. Every report labels FACT, INFERENCE, PLAN, and UNKNOWN and records the four constitutional effects: autonomous, global, irreplaceable-value, and transparency.
