## 1. Strategic decision

KIDULTS will not compete on raw record count, marketplace liquidity or a single-category price guide.

KIDULTS will own the **governed intelligence and decision layer for global collectibles**:

> External sources provide observations. KIDULTS owns object identity, evidence admission, rights interpretation, explainable comparables, confidence, rankability, governed Projections and decision history.

The target competitive position is:

> **The trusted operating layer that turns fragmented collectible evidence into explainable object and market decisions for collectors and institutions.**

This position complements strong data partners while preventing any provider from becoming the system of record for KIDULTS intelligence.

## 2. Where KIDULTS will and will not compete

### 2.1 Chosen competitive arenas

KIDULTS will compete on:

1. canonical object identity across heterogeneous sources;
2. object-level intelligence beyond price history;
3. evidence, provenance, freshness and rights traceability;
4. explainable comparable selection and distance;
5. explicit confidence and independent rankability;
6. governed Projection delivery across product surfaces and APIs;
7. one evidence base expressed through Collector and Institutional decision lenses;
8. cross-vertical methodology and Kidult 100 benchmark governance;
9. provider switching without changing the customer-facing analytical contract;
10. accumulated object, evidence, decision and outcome lineage.

### 2.2 Arenas deliberately not chosen

KIDULTS will not attempt to win through:

- the largest raw transaction database;
- proprietary marketplace liquidity;
- grading authority without separate authorization;
- opaque automated valuation;
- unsupported real-time claims;
- a generic AI chat interface;
- record-count marketing without relevance and admissibility;
- one provider's schema, score or brand becoming the KIDULTS product;
- index publication before evidence, representativeness and rankability approval.

## 3. Competitive thesis

### 3.1 Current structural advantage

KIDULTS has designed a differentiated chain:

`Observation → Canonical Object → Evidence Admission → Independent Validation → Governed Projection → Decision Lens → Audit History`

The innovation is system-level, not cosmetic:

- Evidence Before Metrics;
- missing is not zero;
- VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNAVAILABLE / RIGHTS BLOCKED / STALE state semantics;
- Confidence is distinct from Rankability;
- Projection is the only product-facing intelligence contract;
- Collector and Institution use the same approved evidence but different decision grammar;
- generated/editorial imagery is never market evidence;
- every product surface can reach provenance and Governance records.

### 3.2 Current market disadvantage

KIDULTS does not yet possess sufficient approved market evidence, live Projections, independently validated analytical accuracy, paying design-partner outcomes or recurring revenue evidence.

Therefore:

> **Current advantage = architecture and method.  
> Target advantage = validated accuracy, customer outcomes, recurring use and compounding decision lineage.**

No team may describe structural readiness as proven market leadership.

## 4. Competitiveness maturity model

A single subjective score is prohibited as the official gate. Market competitiveness advances only through observable evidence.

| Level | Meaning | Required exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **C0 — Concept** | Market problem and product idea defined | Category, customer, problem and exclusion boundary documented |
| **C1 — Product Contract** | Analytical grammar and product states defined | Positioning, Projection schema, state model, UX contracts and claims policy versioned |
| **C2 — Evidence Ready** | Lawful evidence can enter the governed chain | Authorized sources, canonicalization, lineage, rights, freshness and immutable Candidate/Evidence pair |
| **C3 — Validated Pilot** | Product produces defensible results for bounded use cases | Track B assessment, explainable comparable validation, approved Projection, pilot QA and human acceptance |
| **C4 — Market Ready** | Customers can buy and repeatedly use the product | Paid design partners, entitlement and support model, retention/usage evidence, operating reliability and explicit G5 |
| **C5 — Category Leadership** | KIDULTS becomes a reference layer across categories | Multi-provider resilience, benchmark adoption, partner integrations, recurring revenue and independently evidenced trust |

**Current official state:** C1 foundation materially established; C2 is not complete.  
**Target:** achieve C4 before any broad market-leadership claim; build C5 through compounding evidence, integrations and benchmark adoption.

Use where domain depth materially improves a Product × Market Cell. No specialist's category score becomes a universal KIDULTS score without independent methodology and validation.

Treat their observations as venue-specific evidence. Marketplace liquidity, listing price and sold result must remain semantically distinct.

### AI and technology partners

Models and connectors may accelerate extraction, mapping and explanation. They may not independently admit evidence, change a verified state, approve rankability, publish an index or authorize Production.
