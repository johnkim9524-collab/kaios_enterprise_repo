# Dual Identity Methodology v1

**ID:** `dual-identity-methodology-v1`  
**Status:** REGISTERED FOR INTERNAL VALIDATION  
**Effective:** 2026-08-16  
**Owner:** Track A

## Identity Layers

1. `source_record_identity` — a source-qualified raw record.
2. `physical_object_identity` — one accession-, serial-, or lot-specific object.
3. `canonical_design_identity` — maker/designer + design/model + version + date range.
4. `market_event_identity` — one listing, auction or private-sale event.
5. `evidence_assertion_identity` — one claim tied to provenance and observation time.

## Non-negotiable Rule

Physical-object equality must not be inferred from title similarity alone.

## Structural Candidate Rule

The first internal Candidate may preserve source records without merging them into canonical designs. A Golden Dataset is required before automated canonical-design entity resolution can be treated as verified.
